# AMO listing — English (default)

## Name

Audio Extender

## Summary

Volume booster up to 600% without distortion, studio-grade equalizer, headphone correction and one-click sound fixes for every tab. Free, no ads, no tracking.

## Description

```html
Too quiet video? Voices lost behind the music? Headphones that sound flat? <b>Audio Extender</b> makes any website sound better — YouTube, Twitch, podcasts, online courses, browser games and more.

<b>🔊 Up to 600% louder</b>
Raise the volume far beyond 100%. A built-in limiter keeps it clean: louder, but never crackling or distorted.

<b>🎚️ Studio-grade equalizer</b>
Drag points on a live frequency curve to boost bass, bring out vocals or tame harsh highs. Prefer sliders? Switch to 10 or 31 bands. Ready-made presets (Bass Boost, Vocal, Podcast, Rock and more) — and save your own.

<b>🎧 Headphone correction</b>
Pick your model from the AutoEq database of thousands of headphones and hear them the way they should sound.

<b>✨ Fix the sound with one switch</b>
<ul><li><b>Clear dialogue</b> — understand voices in films and streams even when the music is loud</li><li><b>Night mode</b> — quiet scenes louder, explosions quieter</li><li><b>Bass boost</b> and <b>stereo width</b> — more punch and a wider stage</li><li><b>Normalization</b> — every video plays at a similar loudness</li><li><b>Mono</b> and <b>left/right balance</b> — for one earbud or hearing on one side</li><li><b>Playback speed</b> — faster lectures and podcasts without chipmunk voices</li></ul>
<b>🎛️ Every tab under control</b>
The Mixer shows all tabs playing sound: set each one's volume, mute it, play one solo, or lower other tabs automatically while you watch something.

<b>💾 Remembers every site</b>
Settings are saved per site and stay after a browser restart.

<b>🎨 Make it yours</b>
5 themes (Neon, Cyber, Sunset, Aurora, Light), 6 languages, popup or sidebar, keyboard shortcuts: <code>Alt+Shift+A</code> open, <code>Alt+Shift+↑/↓</code> volume, <code>Alt+Shift+B</code> on/off.

<b>🔒 Private by design</b>
No data collection, no ads, no analytics. All sound processing happens locally in your browser. The only network request: downloading the public AutoEq database when you search for your headphones.

<b>Good to know</b>
Firefox doesn't allow extensions on its own pages (settings, PDF viewer, addons.mozilla.org). DRM-protected audio (for example Spotify or Netflix) can't be changed by any extension — it keeps playing normally, just without effects.

Open source (GPL-3.0): <a href="https://github.com/AlexeyKopkin/Audio-Extender">github.com/AlexeyKopkin/Audio-Extender</a>
Enjoying it? <a href="https://buymeacoffee.com/kotx">Buy me a coffee</a> ☕
```
